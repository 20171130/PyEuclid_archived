Auxiliary constructions:
m = construct_midpoint(f, g)
Solution:
1. Collinear(c,d,f) => Parallel(c,d,c,f)
2. Perpendicular(a,f,c,d) & Parallel(c,d,c,f) => Perpendicular(a,f,c,f)
3. Collinear(c,d,f) & Collinear(c,d,g) => Collinear(c,f,g)
4. Collinear(c,f,g) => Parallel(c,f,f,g)
5. Perpendicular(a,f,c,f) & Parallel(c,f,f,g) => Perpendicular(a,f,f,g)
6. Perpendicular(a,f,c,d) & Perpendicular(b,g,c,d) => Parallel(a,f,b,g)
7. Length_f_m - Length_g_m & Length_a_e - Length_b_e => Length_a_e/Length_b_e - Length_f_m/Length_g_m
8. Collinear(a,b,e) & Parallel(a,f,b,g) & Collinear(f,g,m) & Length_a_e/Length_b_e - Length_f_m/Length_g_m => Parallel(a,f,e,m)
9. Perpendicular(a,f,f,g) & Parallel(a,f,e,m) => Perpendicular(e,m,f,g)
10. Midpoint(m,f,g) & Perpendicular(e,m,f,g) => Length_e_f - Length_e_g
