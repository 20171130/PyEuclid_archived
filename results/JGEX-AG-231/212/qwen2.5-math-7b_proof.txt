Auxiliary constructions:
k = construct_midpoint(a, g)
Solution:
1. Collinear(c,d,f) => Parallel(c,d,c,f)
2. Perpendicular(a,f,c,d) & Parallel(c,d,c,f) => Perpendicular(a,f,c,f)
3. Collinear(c,d,f) & Collinear(c,d,g) => Collinear(c,f,g)
4. Collinear(c,f,g) => Parallel(c,f,f,g)
5. Perpendicular(a,f,c,f) & Parallel(c,f,f,g) => Perpendicular(a,f,f,g)
6. Midpoint(k,a,g) & Perpendicular(a,f,f,g) => Length_f_k - Length_g_k
7. Length_f_k - Length_g_k => IsoscelesTriangle(f,g,k)
8. Perpendicular(b,g,c,d) & Parallel(c,d,c,f) => Perpendicular(b,g,c,f)
9. Perpendicular(b,g,c,f) & Parallel(c,f,f,g) => Perpendicular(b,g,f,g)
10. Midpoint(e,a,b) & Midpoint(k,a,g) => Parallel(b,g,e,k)
11. Perpendicular(b,g,f,g) & Parallel(b,g,e,k) => Perpendicular(e,k,f,g)
12. IsoscelesTriangle(f,g,k) & Length_f_k - Length_g_k & Perpendicular(e,k,f,g) => Length_e_f - Length_e_g
