Auxiliary constructions:
h = construct_midpoint(f, g)
Solution:
1. Collinear(c,d,f) => Parallel(c,d,c,f)
2. Perpendicular(a,f,c,d) & Parallel(c,d,c,f) => Perpendicular(a,f,c,f)
3. Collinear(c,d,f) & Collinear(c,d,g) => Collinear(c,f,g)
4. Collinear(c,f,g) => Parallel(c,f,f,g)
5. Perpendicular(a,f,c,f) & Parallel(c,f,f,g) => Perpendicular(a,f,f,g)
6. Perpendicular(a,f,c,d) & Perpendicular(b,g,c,d) => Parallel(a,f,b,g)
7. Length_a_e - Length_b_e & Length_f_h - Length_g_h => Length_a_e/Length_b_e - Length_f_h/Length_g_h
8. Collinear(a,b,e) & Parallel(a,f,b,g) & Collinear(f,g,h) & Length_a_e/Length_b_e - Length_f_h/Length_g_h => Parallel(a,f,e,h)
9. Perpendicular(a,f,f,g) & Parallel(a,f,e,h) => Perpendicular(e,h,f,g)
10. Midpoint(h,f,g) & Perpendicular(e,h,f,g) => Length_e_f - Length_e_g
