Solution:
1. Collinear(b,e,f) => Parallel(b,e,b,f)
2. Perpendicular(a,c,b,e) & Parallel(b,e,b,f) => Perpendicular(a,c,b,f)
3. Perpendicular(a,c,b,f) => Angle_a_b_f + Angle_b_a_c - pi/2
4. Collinear(a,d,f) => Parallel(a,d,a,f)
5. Perpendicular(a,d,b,c) & Parallel(a,d,a,f) => Perpendicular(a,f,b,c)
6. Perpendicular(a,f,b,c) => Angle_a_b_c + Angle_b_a_f - pi/2
7. Angle_a_b_f + Angle_b_a_c - pi/2 & Angle_a_b_c + Angle_b_a_f - pi/2 & Angle_a_b_c - Angle_b_a_c => Angle_a_b_f - Angle_b_a_f
8. -Angle_a_b_f + Angle_b_a_f => Length_a_f - Length_b_f
