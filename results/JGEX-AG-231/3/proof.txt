Solution:
1. Length_b_o - Length_c_o => IsoscelesTriangle(b,c,o)
2. IsoscelesTriangle(b,c,o) & Length_b_o - Length_c_o & Collinear(b,c,q) & Midpoint(q,b,c) => Perpendicular(b,c,o,q)
3. Length_j_m - Length_j_o => Midpoint(j,m,o)
4. Midpoint(j,m,o) & Midpoint(j,q,s) => Parallel(m,s,o,q)
5. Perpendicular(b,c,o,q) & Parallel(m,s,o,q) => Perpendicular(b,c,m,s)
