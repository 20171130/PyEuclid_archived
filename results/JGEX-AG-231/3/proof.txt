Solution:
1. Length_b_o + Length_c_o*(-1) => Length_b_o - Length_c_o
2. Length_b_o - Length_c_o => IsoscelesTriangle(b,c,o)
3. IsoscelesTriangle(b,c,o) & Length_b_o - Length_c_o & Collinear(b,c,q) & Midpoint(q,b,c) => Perpendicular(b,c,o,q)
4. Length_j_m + Length_j_o*(-1) => Length_j_m - Length_j_o
5. Length_j_m - Length_j_o & Collinear(j,m,o) => Midpoint(j,m,o)
6. Midpoint(j,m,o) & Midpoint(j,q,s) => Parallel(m,s,o,q)
7. Perpendicular(b,c,o,q) & Parallel(m,s,o,q) => Perpendicular(b,c,m,s)
