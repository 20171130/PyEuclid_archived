Solution:
1. Collinear(a,d,e) & -Length_a_b + Length_a_d & Length_a_d - Length_a_e => Perpendicular(b,d,b,e)
2. Collinear(c,d,f) & -Length_b_c + Length_c_d & Length_c_d - Length_c_f => Perpendicular(b,d,b,f)
3. Perpendicular(b,d,b,e) & Perpendicular(b,d,b,f) => Parallel(b,e,b,f)
4. Parallel(b,e,b,f) => Collinear(b,e,f)
