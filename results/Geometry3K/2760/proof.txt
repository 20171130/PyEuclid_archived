Solution:
1. Parallelogram(J,K,L,M) => Trapezoid(J,K,L,M) & Length_J_K - Length_L_M
2. Collinear(E,L,M) => Parallel(E,L,L,M)
3. Perpendicular(E,J,E,L) & Parallel(E,L,L,M) => Perpendicular(E,J,L,M)
4. Trapezoid(J,K,L,M) & Parallel(J,K,L,M) & Perpendicular(E,J,L,M) & Collinear(E,L,M) => Area_J_K_L_M - Length_E_J*(Length_J_K + Length_L_M)/2
5. Length_L_M - 4 & Length_J_K - Length_L_M => Length_J_K - 4
6. Area_J_K_L_M - Length_E_J*(Length_J_K + Length_L_M)/2 & Length_L_M - 4 & Length_J_K - 4 & Length_E_J - 6 => Area_J_K_L_M - 24
