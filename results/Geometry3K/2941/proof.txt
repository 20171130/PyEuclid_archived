Solution:
1. Length_C_X - Variable_radius_X & Length_D_X - Variable_radius_X => Length_C_X - Length_D_X
2. Length_C_X - Length_D_X => IsoscelesTriangle(C,D,X)
3. Collinear(N,X,Z) => Parallel(N,Z,X,Z)
4. Perpendicular(D,N,N,Z) & Parallel(N,Z,X,Z) => Perpendicular(D,N,X,Z)
5. Collinear(C,D,N) => Parallel(C,D,D,N)
6. Perpendicular(D,N,X,Z) & Parallel(C,D,D,N) => Perpendicular(C,D,X,Z)
7. IsoscelesTriangle(C,D,X) & Length_C_X - Length_D_X & Perpendicular(C,D,X,Z) => Angle_C_X_Z - Angle_D_X_Z
8. Angle_C_X_Z - Angle_D_X_Z & Angle_C_X_Z - 2*pi/9 => Angle_D_X_Z - 2*pi/9
9. Angle_C_X_D - Angle_C_X_Z - Angle_D_X_Z & Angle_D_X_Z - 2*pi/9 & Angle_C_X_Z - 2*pi/9 => Angle_C_X_D - 4*pi/9
