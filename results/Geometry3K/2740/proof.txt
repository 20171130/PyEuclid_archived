Solution:
1. Perpendicular(A,E,C,E) => Angle_A_E_C - pi/2
2. Length_A_C**2 - Length_A_E**2 + 2*Length_A_E*Length_C_E*cos(Angle_A_E_C) - Length_C_E**2 & Length_A_C - 17 & Angle_A_E_C - pi/2 & Length_C_E - 8 => Length_A_E - 15
3. Parallelogram(A,B,D,C) => Trapezoid(A,B,D,C) & Parallel(A,B,C,D) & Length_A_B - Length_C_D
4. Collinear(C,D,E) => Parallel(C,D,C,E)
5. Perpendicular(A,E,C,E) & Parallel(C,D,C,E) => Perpendicular(A,E,C,D)
6. Trapezoid(A,B,D,C) & Parallel(A,B,C,D) & Perpendicular(A,E,C,D) & Collinear(C,D,E) => Area_A_B_D_C - Length_A_E*(Length_A_B + Length_C_D)/2
7. Length_A_B - Length_C_D & Length_A_B - 21 => Length_C_D - 21
8. Area_A_B_D_C - Length_A_E*(Length_A_B + Length_C_D)/2 & Length_C_D - 21 & Length_A_B - 21 => Area_A_B_D_C - 21*Length_A_E
9. Length_A_E - 15 & Area_A_B_D_C - 21*Length_A_E => Area_A_B_D_C - 315
