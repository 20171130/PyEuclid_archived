Solution:
1. Length_A_C - Length_B_D & Length_A_D - Length_B_C => Parallelogram(A,C,B,D)
2. Parallelogram(A,C,B,D) => Parallel(A,D,B,C)
3. Perpendicular(A,D,B,D) & Parallel(A,D,B,C) => Perpendicular(B,C,B,D)
4. Perpendicular(B,C,B,D) => Angle_C_B_D - pi/2
5. Length_A_C - Length_A_D & Length_A_D - Length_B_C => Length_A_C - Length_B_C
6. Length_A_C - Length_B_D & Length_A_C - Length_A_D & Length_A_D - Length_B_C => Length_B_C - Length_B_D
7. Length_A_C - Length_B_D & Length_A_C - Length_A_D => Length_A_D - Length_B_D
8. Length_A_C - Length_B_C & Length_B_C - Length_B_D & -Length_A_D + Length_B_D => Rhombus(A,C,B,D)
9. Rhombus(A,C,B,D) => Angle_A_B_C - Angle_A_B_D
10. Angle_C_B_D - pi/2 & Angle_A_B_C + Angle_A_B_D - Angle_C_B_D & Angle_A_B_C - Angle_A_B_D => Angle_A_B_C - pi/4
11. Angle_A_B_C - pi/4 & Angle_A_B_C - pi*Variable_y/180 => Variable_y - 45
