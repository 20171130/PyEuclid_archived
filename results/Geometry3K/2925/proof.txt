Solution:
1. Perpendicular(A,B,A,C) => Angle_B_A_C - pi/2
2. Angle_B_A_C - pi/2 => Length_A_C/Length_B_C - sin(Angle_A_B_C)
3. Angle_B_A_C - pi/2 & Angle_A_B_C + Angle_A_C_B + Angle_B_A_C - pi => Angle_A_B_C + Angle_A_C_B - pi/2
4. Length_A_B**2 - 2*Length_A_B*Length_A_C*cos(Angle_B_A_C) + Length_A_C**2 - Length_B_C**2 & Angle_B_A_C - pi/2 & Length_B_C - 5 & Length_A_B - 3 => Length_A_C - 4
5. Length_A_C/Length_B_C - sin(Angle_A_B_C) & Length_B_C - 5 & Angle_A_B_C + Angle_A_C_B - pi/2 & Length_A_C - 4 => Angle_A_C_B - acos(4/5)
6. Angle_A_C_B - acos(4/5) => cos(Angle_A_C_B) - 4/5
