1. Perpendicular(R,S,S,T) => Angle_R_S_T - pi/2
2. Angle_R_S_T - pi/2 & Angle_R_S_T + Angle_R_T_S + Angle_S_R_T - pi => Angle_R_T_S + Angle_S_R_T - pi/2

4. Angle_R_T_S + Angle_S_R_T - pi/2 & Angle_S_R_T - atan(5/2) => Angle_R_T_S - pi/2 + atan(5/2)
