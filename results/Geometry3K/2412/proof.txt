Solution:
1. Length_B_C - 5 & Length_A_B - 5 => Length_A_B - Length_B_C
2. Length_A_B - Length_B_C & Collinear(A,B,C) => Midpoint(B,A,C)
3. Midpoint(B,A,C) => Length_A_C - 2*Length_B_C
4. Length_B_C - 5 & Length_A_C - 2*Length_B_C => Length_A_C - 10
5. Length_B_C - 5 & Length_A_C - 10 => (pi*(Length_A_C**2 - 2*Length_B_C**2) - 50*pi)/pi
