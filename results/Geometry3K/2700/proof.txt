Solution:
1. Length_C_X - Variable_radius_X & Length_D_X - Variable_radius_X => Length_C_X - Length_D_X
2. Length_C_X - Length_D_X => IsoscelesTriangle(C,D,X)
3. Collinear(N,X,Z) => Parallel(N,X,N,Z)
4. Perpendicular(D,N,N,Z) & Parallel(N,X,N,Z) => Perpendicular(D,N,N,X)
5. Collinear(C,D,N) => Parallel(C,D,D,N)
6. Perpendicular(D,N,N,X) & Parallel(C,D,D,N) => Perpendicular(C,D,N,X)
7. IsoscelesTriangle(C,D,X) & Length_C_X - Length_D_X & Collinear(C,D,N) & Perpendicular(C,D,N,X) => Midpoint(N,C,D)
8. Midpoint(N,C,D) => Length_C_D - 2*Length_D_N
9. Length_C_D - 30 & Length_C_D - 2*Length_D_N => Length_D_N - 15
