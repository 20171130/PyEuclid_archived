Solution:
1. Rectangle(A,B,E,D) => Trapezoid(A,B,E,D) & Length_A_D - Length_B_E
2. Perpendicular(A,D,D,E) & Perpendicular(B,E,D,E) => Parallel(A,D,B,E)
3. Trapezoid(A,B,E,D) & Parallel(A,D,B,E) & Perpendicular(A,D,D,E) => Area_A_B_E_D - Length_D_E*(Length_A_D + Length_B_E)/2
4. Length_A_D - Length_B_E & Length_A_D - 5 => Length_B_E - 5
5. Area_A_B_E_D - Length_D_E*(Length_A_D + Length_B_E)/2 & Length_A_D - 5 & Length_D_E - 6 & Length_B_E - 5 => Area_A_B_E_D - 30
6. Area_A_B_C**2 - (-Length_A_B/2 + Length_A_C/2 + Length_B_C/2)*(Length_A_B/2 - Length_A_C/2 + Length_B_C/2)*(Length_A_B/2 + Length_A_C/2 - Length_B_C/2)*(Length_A_B/2 + Length_A_C/2 + Length_B_C/2) & Length_A_C - 4 & Length_A_B - 6 & Length_B_C - 4 => Area_A_B_C - 3*sqrt(7)
7. Area_A_B_E_D - 30 & Area_A_B_C - 3*sqrt(7) => Area_A_B_C - Area_A_B_E_D - 3*sqrt(7) + 30
