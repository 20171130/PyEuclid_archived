Solution:
1. Length_W_X - Length_X_Y => IsoscelesTriangle(W,X,Y)
2. Length_W_X - Length_X_Y & -Length_W_Z + Length_Y_Z => Kite(W,X,Y,Z)
3. Kite(W,X,Y,Z) & Length_W_X - Length_X_Y & -Length_W_Z + Length_Y_Z => Perpendicular(W,Y,X,Z)
4. Collinear(P,X,Z) => Parallel(P,X,X,Z)
5. Perpendicular(W,Y,X,Z) & Parallel(P,X,X,Z) => Perpendicular(P,X,W,Y)
6. IsoscelesTriangle(W,X,Y) & Length_W_X - Length_X_Y & Collinear(P,W,Y) & Perpendicular(P,X,W,Y) => Midpoint(P,W,Y)
7. Midpoint(P,W,Y) => Length_P_W - Length_W_Y/2
8. Collinear(P,W,Y) => Parallel(P,W,W,Y)
9. Perpendicular(P,X,W,Y) & Parallel(P,W,W,Y) => Perpendicular(P,W,P,X)
10. Perpendicular(P,W,P,X) => Angle_W_P_X - pi/2
11. Length_P_W**2 - 2*Length_P_W*Length_P_X*cos(Angle_W_P_X) + Length_P_X**2 - Length_W_X**2 & Length_P_W - Length_W_Y/2 & Angle_W_P_X - pi/2 & Length_P_X - 4 & Length_W_X - 6 => Length_W_Y - 4*sqrt(5)
12. Length_P_W - Length_W_Y/2 & Length_W_Y - 4*sqrt(5) => Length_P_W - 2*sqrt(5)
