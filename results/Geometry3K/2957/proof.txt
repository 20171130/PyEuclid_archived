1. Collinear(A,C,E) & Collinear(B,D,E) & Parallel(A,B,C,D) => Similar3(A,B,E,C,D,E)
2. Similar3(A,B,E,C,D,E) => Length_A_B/Length_C_D - Length_A_E/Length_C_E
3. Length_C_D - 10 & Length_C_E - 8 => -4/5 + Length_C_E/Length_C_D
4. Length_A_B/Length_C_D - Length_A_E/Length_C_E & -4/5 + Length_C_E/Length_C_D => Length_A_B - 5*Length_A_E/4
5. Length_A_B - 5*Length_A_E/4 & Length_A_E - 5 => Length_A_B - 25/4
